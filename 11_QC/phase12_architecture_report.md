## Article Scientific Thesis

The literature strongly resolves transient underexpanded-wave formation, definition-complete canonical compressible droplet response, bounded collective effects, and injection-timing-controlled HPDI ignition as component problems. It does not directly close the full underexpanded-shock → pilot-droplet loading → dense-spray breakup/evaporation → mixture redistribution → ignition/combustion chain under a common HPDI condition.

The review is therefore designed to unify resolved component physics while visibly separating direct evidence, mixed/indirect support, cross-scale inference, and evidence gaps.

## Article Narrative

```text
CH01 establishes the thesis and evidence discipline
→ CH02 defines the transient injection and underexpanded-wave boundary
→ CH03 translates topology toward local loading and exposes MP-033
→ CH04 resolves canonical deformation, instability, and breakup
→ CH05 adds collective corrections and exposes MP-032
→ CH06 follows fragments and phase change to the open mixture bridge MP-028
→ CH07 presents direct HPDI timing-to-ignition evidence and exposes MP-029
→ CH08 uses detonation/RDE as a bounded strong-wave analogue
→ CH09 reassembles closures, gaps, and evidence-derived research priorities
```

This order follows mechanism progression rather than the A/B/C/D library structure. Strong-wave analogues are placed after the direct HPDI application evidence so that they cannot silently substitute for it.

## Quantitative Synthesis Feasibility

### Moderate feasibility

1. Definition-complete NPR versus x_MD/D under compatible gas/nozzle/ambient/transient conditions.
2. M_s or M_rel plus definition-complete We versus conditioned deformation/breakup response.
3. d0 versus breakup/evaporation response in separated, condition-matched domains.
4. Signed ΔSOI versus mixture/ignition response in condition-faceted HPDI subsets.

These analyses require a later case-level eligibility audit before production.

### Low feasibility

1. Event-aligned p_inj(t) versus Mach-disk motion or stabilization.
2. Typed loading duration or the project-hypothesis τ_loading/τ_response versus breakup state.
3. S/D or typed cloud descriptors versus collective response.
4. Typed L_E/L_D versus RDE behavior.

These are more likely to support small multiples, within-study panels, or qualitative condition maps than pooled cross-paper plots.

### Not recommended

1. Generic NPR versus shock metrics.
2. Generic Mach/We versus a universal breakup regime.
3. Generic breakup time versus loading variables.
4. RDE L_E/L_D mapped directly to HPDI ignition or combustion.

No definition standard was relaxed to increase plot density.

## Core Review Contributions

1. Unify transient underexpanded-jet, compressible droplet, collective spray, phase-change, and HPDI ignition literature in one mechanism progression.
2. Establish definition discipline for NPR, Mach, We/Re/Oh, loading duration, breakup time, ΔSOI, and characteristic-scale comparisons.
3. Separate direct, mixed/indirect, cross-scale, and missing evidence without upgrading application claims.
4. Identify and structurally deploy MP-033, MP-032, MP-028, and MP-029 as the four main unclosed HPDI bridges.
5. Treat fragment populations and collective effects as required mediators between canonical breakup and engine mixture formation.
6. Convert evidence gaps into section-specific measurement, modelling, and reporting priorities.

## Most Important Writing Cautions

1. Cross-scale support must not be rewritten as a measured end-to-end HPDI mechanism.
2. NPR comparisons require matched pressure roles, types, locations, time bases, and transient states.
3. We/Mach comparisons require full reference definitions, loading history, d0, and liquid context.
4. Single-droplet or ordered-droplet response must not be transferred directly to dense HPDI spray.
5. RDE/detonation evidence is analogue-only for HPDI transfer.
6. Direct timing-to-ignition evidence does not close shock → breakup → mixture → ignition causality.
7. Phase change cannot be assigned one universal influence on breakup.
8. Paper-conditioned combustion claims must remain localized.
