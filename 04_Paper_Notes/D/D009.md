# D009

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

- Title: On the propagation stability of droplet-laden two-phase rotating detonation waves
- Paper ID / source record: D009 / D-RDE-04
- Year / DOI: 2022 / 10.1016/j.combustflame.2022.112271
- Original PDF: `02_PDF_Raw/D/D-RDE-04_2022_Wen_DropletLaden_RotatingDetonation_PropagationStability.pdf`

## 2. Pilot Role

RDE characteristic-scale ratio and propagation-stability benchmark.

## 3. Research Question

Can droplet evaporation distance relative to detonation-front height organize the stability boundary of a two-phase rotating detonation?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Analytical stability criterion plus 2D Eulerian–Lagrangian reactive simulations of kerosene droplets and hot air.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

Unrolled annular combustor, ideal premixing, dilute uniform droplets, D² evaporation law; total inlet pressure 700 kPa for main cases.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

Simulation family uses total equivalence ratio φ_t=1, droplets d0=2–5 μm for no-pre-evaporation cases, and φ_pre=0.2–0.6 at d0=5 μm.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Rotating detonation front with transverse waves/TDWs, unburned pockets and shock–flame decoupling near quench.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

Uniform initial kerosene droplet diameter; dilute volume fraction assumption <1.26×10^-4; zero initial gas–droplet relative velocity.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

Δ=L_E/L_D; L_E is evaporation distance, L_D detonation-front height. Under assumptions, Δ=(κ/(L_y/u_D)) d0².

## 10. Time Scales

Evaporation time enters L_E=ūt_E. Tracked examples at d0=4 μm survive 7.6–21 μs depending on front-relative release location.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Increasing d0 increases Δ, enlarges low-equivalence/unburned regions and destabilizes propagation; pre-evaporation restores stability by raising upstream gaseous fuel.

## 12. Quantitative Results

At φ_pre=0: d0=2,3,4 μm yield Δ=0.13,0.31,0.55 and velocities 1728,1696,1618 m/s; d0=5 μm gives Δ=0.84 and fails. Stability fit gives Δ≤0.72 without pre-evaporation.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Author model: incomplete evaporation lowers local φ; transverse detonations may consume pockets until shock/flame decouple and quench.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Droplet scale d0 → evaporation time/distance L_E → scale ratio L_E/L_D → local equivalence distribution → TDW/unburned pockets → propagation stability.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

Reactive kerosene/air rotating detonation with reduced species mechanism and two-way phase coupling.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Criterion/definitions pp.2–4; numerical method pp.5–9; cases/results pp.10–14; stability boundary and conclusions p.15.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

Δ and criterion are author-reported analytical constructs; simulations validate only the tested idealized family; broader cross-scale use is a project synthesis candidate.

## 18. Limitations

2D, uniform droplets, ideal premixing, D² law, fixed chemistry/inlet family; other criterion parameters not swept.

## 19. Evidence Candidates

- PILOT-D009-C02: For the simulated no-pre-evaporation family, increasing Δ produces larger unburned pockets and eventual shock–flame decoupling. [PDF p.12, Table 3 and quench discussion]
