# D018

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

D018 / D-DW-03. Identity verified from canonical PDF.

## 2. Tier 1 Role

Primary detonation-driven RP-3/water breakup evidence.

## 3. Research Question

How do liquid properties and detonation loading alter deformation, acceleration, breakup mechanism and timescale?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Detonation-tube high-speed shadowgraph experiments with RP-3 and water.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

Droplets 0.25-1.27 mm; Ma 6.03-7.07; 15 tabulated cases.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

Ethylene plus 50% O2 oxygen-enriched air, equivalence ratio 0.9; post-detonation state uses named ZND/CJ averaging.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Stable detonation front; post-front density and velocity gradients distinguish loading from a uniform post-shock state.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

Single water or RP-3 droplets; surface tension 0.0728 and 0.0245 N/m.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

We uses averaged post-detonation rho_g and ug; Oh definition and all case values preserved; Mach is detonation-front/laboratory-frame.

## 10. Time Scales

t*=t ug/d0 sqrt(rho_g/rho_l); B4 breakup observed 35-40 us; collapsed t*b 10.06 water and 7.90 RP-3.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Both liquids show KHI stripping followed by coupled KHI-RTI piercing; RP-3 wake also contains a qualitative combustion-related signature.

## 12. Quantitative Results

dc/d0=1+0.72821t*; Cd=0.23; x/d0=0.08054(t*)^2; breakup-time constants differ by liquid.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Authors attribute slower deformation/acceleration than shocks to the adverse post-detonation pressure gradient and longer breakup to lower average dynamic pressure.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Detonation -> KHI stripping -> RT piercing/coupling -> catastrophic breakup; adverse gradient weakens acceleration.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

RP-3 vapor may burn in the hot wake, but shadowgraph evidence is qualitative and not stored as a quantitative combustion parameter.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Table 1; Eqs.1-11; Figs.4-18; Conclusions.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

All values are paper-reported measurements/correlations; no new derived values.

## 18. Limitations

Projection imaging, finite resolution, engineering ZND averaging; shock comparison partly literature-secondary.

## 19. Evidence Candidates

T1-D018-C01 through C03.

## 20. Contradictions / Comparison Hooks

Directly contrasts detonation versus shock loading without forcing equivalence.

## 21. Parameter-Schema Gaps

No blocking gap. The qualitative reacting wake is preserved in prose rather than promoted to numeric evidence.

## 22. Relevance to Review Mainline

Detonation characteristic loading, breakup mechanism and timescale.
