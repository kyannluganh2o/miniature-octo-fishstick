# A016

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

- Title: Investigations of diesel and natural gas injection interaction on combustion characteristics of a high-pressure direct-injection dual-fuel engine based on large eddy simulation
- Paper ID / source record: A016 / A-NG-16

## 2. Pilot Role

LES evidence-chain test from jet interaction through vortex/heat/radical transport to ignition and combustion.

## 3. Research Question

How does diesel–natural-gas jet intersection alter coherent flow, radical transport and methane ignition in HPDI operation?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

3D engine LES in CONVERGE with DMD and detailed chemistry; auxiliary CHEMKIN pathway calculations.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

1/9 periodic sector of nine-hole coaxial dual-fuel injector; varied diesel angle creates β=4°,2°,0°,-2°,-4° relative to fixed natural-gas angle.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

1300 rpm full load baseline, IMEP 26.35 bar; diesel/natural-gas injection pressures 30/28 MPa; wall temperatures 553/523/420 K.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

NA as an imposed shock; high-pressure jets create unsteady vortical flow in-cylinder.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

Diesel spray uses KH-RT breakup, Frossling evaporation and collision/wall-film submodels; methane enters through mass-flow INFLOW boundary.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

No core We/Mach extraction; intersection angle β and engine crank-angle phase are primary contexts.

## 10. Time Scales

Diesel precedes natural gas. DMD tracks modes from natural-gas injection to ignition; combustion phases CA10/CA50/CA90 are compared.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Reduced β increases overlap with diesel active-product region, strengthens vortex entrainment and advances natural-gas ignition; β=4° separates the jet from most active products.

## 12. Quantitative Results

β=2° is reported optimal for the studied full-load case and reduces CO by 44.9%. OH addition of 5×10^-5 increases CH4+OH pathway share to 99.6/99.5% at φ=0.5/1.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Resolved DMD/temperature/OH fields plus pathway analysis support vortex entrainment → heat/OH exchange → methane consumption → ignition advance.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Injection intersection → coherent vortices/entrainment → scalar heat and OH transport → CH4 dehydrogenation/oxidation → ignition phase and CO response.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

SAGE detailed chemistry; diesel surrogate five components and methane; 101 species with mechanism reporting differing reaction counts in adjacent text (recorded as a limitation).

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Model pp.3–5; DMD method pp.5–8; angle cases/results pp.9–12; conclusions pp.12–13.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

Flow/species fields are simulation-resolved; causal chain is author interpretation supported by fields; CHEMKIN radical addition is a separate controlled calculation.

## 18. Limitations

Numerical model and sector periodicity; calibration discrepancies; auxiliary OH test is homogeneous and not the full LES field; β optimum is operating-point specific.
