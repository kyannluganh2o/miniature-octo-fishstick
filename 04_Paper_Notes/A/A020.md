# A020

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

- Title: Hydrogen-diesel dual-fuel direct-injection (H2DDI) combustion under compression-ignition engine conditions
- Paper ID / source record: A020 / A-H2-03
- Year / DOI: 2023 / 10.1016/j.ijhydene.2022.09.241
- Original PDF: `02_PDF_Raw/A/A-H2-03_2023_Rorimpandey_H2DDI_Combustion_CI_Conditions.pdf`

## 2. Pilot Role

Multi-event injection sequence and ignition chronology benchmark.

## 3. Research Question

How do injection order, dwell time and ambient temperature alter pilot-to-H2 ignition and flame propagation?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Constant-volume combustion chamber experiment with high-speed schlieren and pressure-derived apparent heat-release rate.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

Two converging single-hole injectors; H2 nozzle 0.58 mm, n-heptane 0.105 mm; axes intersect 57.2 mm from H2 nozzle and jets can overlap from ~28 mm.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

Ambient density 23.8 kg/m³; 890, 820, 780 K at 5.20, 4.60, 4.45 MPa; H2 reservoir 20 MPa (~14 MPa upstream nozzle), n-heptane rail 70 MPa.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

NA — no imposed shock; high-pressure jets interact in a quiescent engine-relevant ambient.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

n-heptane pilot spray plus gaseous H2 jet; injected masses 0.99 and 5.28 mg, respectively.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

No We/Mach core extraction; injection sequence and event-relative time are the primary context.

## 10. Time Scales

Actual-SOI dwell cases: pilot-first 0.93,1.93,2.93 ms; H2-first 1.07,2.07,3.07 ms; near-simultaneous pilot 0.07 ms after H2. Hydraulic durations 0.7/3.3 ms.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Pilot-first long dwell allows burnt pilot products to cool/lean before interaction; H2-first long dwell can move main ignition beyond H2 EOI into a lean region.

## 12. Quantitative Results

Pilot-only schlieren ignition delays: 0.62±0.04 ms at 890 K, 0.93±0.05 ms at 820 K, 1.68±0.12 ms at 780 K.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Authors link successful H2 ignition to duration/location of interaction with reacting pilot products and to the H2 mixture state when that interaction occurs.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Primary SOI → jet penetration/overlap → pilot autoignition → finite interaction with burnt products → H2 ignition → flame propagation/diffusion-burning period.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

Two distinct ignition events; pilot timing from schlieren, main timing from AHRR threshold. Combustion variability rises at lower temperature/very long delays.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Geometry and Tables 1–3 pp.2–4; ignition definitions p.7; results pp.8–15; conclusions p.16.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

Times and imaging observations are reported; mixture-state explanation is author interpretation; no derived relative momentum.

## 18. Limitations

Quiescent CVCC, two single-hole injectors, n-heptane surrogate, schlieren line-of-sight and AHRR threshold definitions.

## 19. Evidence Candidates

- PILOT-A020-C01: Main-fuel ignition follows a finite interaction with reacting/burnt pilot products, and the required interaction duration depends on dwell and charge temperature. [PDF p.16, Conclusions]
- PILOT-A020-C02: Ignition after H2 end of injection produces slower propagation and persistent lean unburned zones. [PDF p.16, Conclusion item 4]
