# A022

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

- Title: Effects of jet interaction angle on the ignition and combustion characteristics of hydrogen-diesel dual-fuel direct injection
- Paper ID / source record: A022 / A-H2-05
- Year / DOI: 2024 / 10.1016/j.ijhydene.2024.04.166

## 2. Pilot Role

Interaction-angle/jet-geometry and relative-momentum schema benchmark.

## 3. Research Question

How do converging angle and ambient oxygen affect pilot ignition and pilot-to-H2 transition?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

CVCC experiment with schlieren and pressure-derived AHRR; H2 and n-heptane single-hole jets.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

Converging relative angles 12°,15°,19°; predicted overlap starts ~21,17,13 mm from H2 nozzle. H2/n-heptane nozzles 0.51/0.105 mm.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

830 K, density 23.8 kg/m³, nominal 4.70 MPa at 12°; ambient O2 21,15,10 vol.%; H2 reservoir 20 MPa and n-heptane 70 MPa.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

NA — interacting jets, not shock loading.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

n-heptane pilot spray; H2 gaseous main jet. Pilot energy share 12%.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

No reported relative-momentum ratio. Interaction angle and injection-duration cases are retained without inventing that parameter.

## 10. Time Scales

H2 starts first; n-heptane SOI 0.6 ms later. Hydraulic durations 4.7 ms H2 and 0.6 ms pilot; pilot-duration sensitivity extends pilot to 1.8 ms.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Greater angle yields earlier overlap and generally shorter pilot-to-main transition, while lower O2 lengthens transition. Pilot ignition can advance or delay depending on whether overlap precedes ignition.

## 12. Quantitative Results

At 12°,15°,19°, overlap begins near 21,17,13 mm. O2 levels 21/15/10 vol.%. Relative momentum is NR as a scalar.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Authors propose relative jet momentum and post-EOI pilot entrainment as contributors to upstream kernel formation; mechanism remains incompletely resolved.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Geometry/angle + injection timing → overlap location and entrainment → pilot ignition shift → pilot-main transition → flame stabilization/AHRR variability.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

At 10% O2, upstream kernels can merge with the downstream reaction zone and change stabilization and heat release.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Geometry/conditions pp.2–4; ignition definitions/results p.5; momentum-duration test p.12; conclusions p.14.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

Angles and overlap distances are reported; relative-momentum role is author interpretation; numeric momentum ratio is NR.

## 18. Limitations

Quiescent CVCC; local temperature compensation at off-center injectors; schlieren cannot resolve species/equivalence ratio after jet merging.

## 19. Evidence Candidates

- PILOT-A022-C02: A numeric relative-momentum ratio is not reported and should not be created from incomplete inputs. [PDF p.12, Sec. 3.4]
