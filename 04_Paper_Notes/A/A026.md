# A026

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

A026 / A-H2-09. Identity verified from canonical PDF.

## 2. Tier 1 Role

Primary H2DDI optical-engine experiment.

## 3. Research Question

How do H2 energy share and electronic SOI alter pilot ignition, H2 flame development and combustion mode?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Single-cylinder optical engine; dual-camera luminosity, pressure and aHRR.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

35 MPa side H2 injector with 1 mm cap; 75 MPa central diesel injector; 1200 rpm.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

H2/diesel injection pressures 350/750 bar; total 1200 J/cycle; intake 101.3 kPa and 303 K.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

No underexpanded-jet shock structure is directly measured; emphasis is in-cylinder jet/flame coupling.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

NA: no droplets; gaseous H2 plus diesel pilot spray/flame.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

Energy shares and crank-angle event definitions are preserved case by case; no NPR inferred.

## 10. Time Scales

Electronic H2 SOI 0-30 CA bTDC and fixed diesel SOI 6 CA bTDC; command durations tabulated.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Larger pilots accelerate early H2 reaction; advanced H2 timing increases premixing but can dilute/delay the pilot.

## 12. Quantitative Results

Table 2 preserves pressures, timings, durations and shares; plotted ignition-delay values are not digitized.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Pilot size controls ignition-source extent; authors attribute the most advanced case's delay to local H2 dilution.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

H2 SOI/diesel SOI -> preignition overlap -> pilot ignition -> H2 propagation -> mixing-controlled burn.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

Directly relevant to mixing, ignition, heat release and flame stabilization.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Tables 1-3; Figs.3-13; Conclusions.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

Reported values only; no curve digitization or inferred numeric ignition delay.

## 18. Limitations

Luminosity is line-of-sight and threshold-sensitive; preignition distributions are not visualized; camera setups differ.
