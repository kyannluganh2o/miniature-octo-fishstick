## 2. Tier 1 Role

Review-level underexpanded-jet framework and confidence audit.

## 3. Research Question

Which geometric, shock-structure, and similarity relations for axisymmetric free underexpanded jets are reliable, and where does the literature remain quantitatively uncertain?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Review centered on experimental literature, with theoretical/numerical context and thermodynamic-model discussion.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

Axisymmetric free jets into quiescent media; convergent and convergent-divergent nozzles are distinguished.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

Pressure definitions are explicit: eta_0=P_0/P_infinity and eta_e=P_e/P_infinity; they are not interchangeable.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Near field contains core and mixing layer; regular reflection yields diamond cells, singular reflection yields a Mach disk/barrel shock, and extreme underexpansion can become first-cell dominated.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

NA: no liquid droplet or spray case is investigated.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

Pressure-ratio and Mach-number definitions are treated contextually; correlations depend on total versus exit pressure ratio, exit Mach number, gamma, and nozzle geometry.

## 10. Time Scales

Steady/quasi-steady spatial structure is reviewed; transient Mach-disk oscillation and hysteresis are noted but not normalized into one duration.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Mach-disk position collapses more consistently than diameter. Far-field profiles show self-similar hyperbolic decay when suitable equivalent-jet scaling is used.

## 12. Quantitative Results

The review retains L_MD/D_e=0.645497 sqrt(eta_0) for convergent nozzles and reports substantially larger uncertainty for diameter and other characteristic lengths.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Increasing pressure ratio is consistently associated with downstream Mach-disk displacement, while nozzle angle, gamma, and exit Mach effects on other quantities are not uniformly resolved.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Pressure-driven expansion -> shock/reflection pattern -> mixing-layer growth -> transition -> far-field self-similarity.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

No reacting or ignition calculation; relevance is through gas-jet mixing and dilution physics.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

All numerical expressions are review-secondary correlations or reviewed ranges. They are not classified as primary experimental values.

## 18. Limitations

Heterogeneous nozzle geometries, fluids, pressure definitions, and limited high-pressure data produce scatter. Curvature, cell disappearance, characteristic lengths, entrainment onset, and mixing-layer transition remain weakly constrained.

## 19. Evidence Candidates

- T1-B004-C01: Mach-disk position is better constrained than other near-field quantities.
- T1-B004-C02: Far-field decay is robust but equivalent-nozzle normalization remains model-dependent.
