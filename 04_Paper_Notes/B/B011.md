# B011

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

- Title: Gas dynamics and flow characteristics of highly turbulent under-expanded hydrogen and methane jets under various nozzle pressure ratios and ambient pressures
- Paper ID / source record: B011 / B-H2-06
- Year / DOI: 2016 / 10.1016/j.ijhydene.2016.02.017
- Original PDF: `02_PDF_Raw/B/B-H2-06_2016_Hamzehloo_GasDynamics_Hydrogen_MethaneJets_NPR.pdf`

## 2. Pilot Role

NPR benchmark: pressure roles, underexpanded shock structure, Mach disk dimensions, shock-cell spacing, gas and ambient-pressure effects.

## 3. Research Question

How do NPR, ambient pressure, and gas species control the transient and quasi-steady structure of underexpanded jets?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Three-dimensional compressible LES of hydrogen and methane jets; nonreacting, single circular nozzle.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

Nozzle diameter D = 1.5 mm; H2 cases at NPR 10 with three ambient pressures, plus H2/CH4 comparison at NPR 8.5.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

NPR is explicitly P0/P∞, with upstream/nozzle total pressure over ambient static pressure. P∞ = 98.37, 491.85, 983.70 kPa for the NPR-10 H2 set.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Starting barrel/reflected-shock system develops into Mach reflection and shock cells. At fixed NPR, near-nozzle quasi-steady geometry is nearly invariant with ambient pressure.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

NA — gas jets without droplets.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

NPR = P0/P∞. Jet-exit Mach is about 1.1; local maximum Mach reaches 3.98 for NPR 10.

## 10. Time Scales

Mach reflection appears 2–3 μs sooner at higher ambient pressure at fixed NPR. Formation/growth and settled structure are kept separate.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Higher ambient pressure changes transient timing but not settled Mach-disk size or shock-cell spacing at fixed NPR. Hydrogen tip vortices promote upstream mixing more strongly than methane.

## 12. Quantitative Results

For H2 NPR 10 at ~1 bar, predicted Mach-disk height 3.09 mm and width 1.34 mm; experiment 3.05 mm and 1.30 mm. Second shock-cell length ~4.04 mm.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Author interpretation: density-weighted shear and coherent vortices explain gas-dependent mixing; equal NPR fixes the principal near-nozzle pressure-adjustment geometry.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Upstream total/ambient static pressure ratio → expansion and Mach reflection → shock cells and tip-vortex organization → mixing response.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

Mixing only; no ignition or combustion model.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Eq./definition of NPR on PDF pp.2–3; validation on p.7; transient development p.8; Mach/shock-cell values p.16; conclusions p.21.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

Numerical fields and quoted dimensions are reported; explanations of vortex-mediated mixing are author interpretations; no project-derived numeric values are used.

## 18. Limitations

LES and idealized single-hole nozzle; nonreacting; fixed supply boundary; conclusions apply to studied pressure ratios and gases.

## 19. Evidence Candidates

- PILOT-B011-C01: At fixed NPR, changing ambient pressure primarily alters starting-transient timing, not the settled near-nozzle shock geometry. [PDF p.21, Conclusions]
- PILOT-B011-C02: Hydrogen exhibits upstream tip-vortex mixing behavior that differs from methane at the same NPR. [PDF p.21, Conclusions]
