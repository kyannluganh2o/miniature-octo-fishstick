# B021

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

- Title: Numerical characterization of hydrogen under-expanded jets with a focus on Internal Combustion Engines applications
- Paper ID / source record: B021 / B-E-01
- Original PDF: `02_PDF_Raw/B/B-E-01_2023_Anaclerio_Numerical_Underexpanded_HydrogenJets_ICE.pdf`

## 2. Tier 1 Role

Tier 1 gas-dynamic anchor for contextual NPR, Mach-disk scaling, ICE transients, thermal diffusion, and multi-hole interaction.

## 3. Research Question

How do NPR, ICE pressure/temperature histories, and jet spacing control hydrogen shock topology and mixing?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Steady/unsteady RANS with ideal and cubic real-gas EoS tests; axisymmetric single jet plus 3D parallel multi-jet cases.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

Convergent 1 mm nozzle for NPR sweep; ICE case uses 0.61 mm holes; multi-jet cases use 1 or 2 mm inter-axis spacing.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

NPR is total plenum/upstream pressure divided by discharge/chamber static pressure. Sweep 2.5-60; ICE upstream 100 bar with chamber 6.15-32.94 bar.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Moderate NPR gives diamond cells; higher NPR gives Mach reflection and Mach disk; very high NPR is first-cell dominated.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

NA: gas-only study; no liquid droplet or spray breakup.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

NPR is contextually linked. Froude values are reported for buoyancy assessment but are not generalized beyond the modeled setup.

## 10. Time Scales

ICE injection lasts 4.9 ms; NPR decreases from 16.24 to 2.97. NPR and Mach-disk histories are registered without curve digitization.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

NPR increases near-field dimensions; temperature affects H2 diffusion rather than Mach-disk position; 1 mm inter-axis jets bend together whereas 2 mm cases do not.

## 12. Quantitative Results

Tables 2-6 provide Mach-disk dimensions, error comparisons, and ICE states; selected endpoints and context are promoted to the master table.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Pressure-ratio-driven expansion establishes shock structure; Soret-enabled thermal diffusion affects far-field mixing; inter-jet expansion waves create a low-pressure attraction region.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

NPR -> expansion/shock topology -> local velocity field -> H2 transport; spacing -> inter-jet wave field -> low pressure -> deflection/coalescence.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

Near-field resolution affects predicted mixture formation; temperature and multi-jet interaction alter hydrogen distribution relevant to ICE combustion.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Boundary conditions p.4; EoS Fig.10 p.7; NPR Eqs.7-9/Tables2-3 pp.7-9; ICE Fig.16/Tables4-6 pp.9-10; hysteresis pp.11-12; temperature p.12; multi-jet pp.13-15.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

All retained values are source-reported simulation inputs/outputs. No pressure basis, external property, or curve point was inferred.

## 18. Limitations

RANS and idealized nozzle geometry; time-history conclusion depends on slow NPR variation; multi-jet study is preliminary and limited to parallel jets at NPR 10.

## 19. Evidence Candidates

## 20. Contradictions / Comparison Hooks

## 21. Parameter-Schema Gaps

## 22. Relevance to Review Mainline
