## 1. Bibliographic Identity

- Title: Effect of the Injector Pressure-Building Process on Dynamic Gas Flow Characteristics of a Highly Turbulent Underexpanded Hydrogen Jet from a Single-Hole Cylindrical Injector

## 2. Pilot Role

Time-dependent injector pressure-building and dynamic-Mach-disk benchmark.

## 3. Research Question

Is a single steady NPR sufficient when injector internal pressure rises over milliseconds?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Pressure measurement and schlieren experiment coupled with 3D LES of an underexpanded H2 jet.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

Modified single-hole injector, D=0.5 mm; background p_b=0.1 MPa, T=300 K; tank pressure 15 MPa; measured internal peak p_in≈9 MPa.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

NPR=p_in/p_b using internal upstream pressure and background pressure; p_in(t) rises over ~4 ms. Absolute/gauge wording is not explicit, so pressure type remains NV.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Mach disk appears around 0.2 ms; shock-cell system develops and joins as pressure builds. Shock dimensions grow then reach turning/stable behavior.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

NA — gas jet.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

Time-varying NPR reaches about 90 at p_in=9 MPa and p_b=0.1 MPa. Correlation correction coefficient 0.85–0.90 at high NPR.

## 10. Time Scales

Measured/real build case turns near 1.1 ms; faster synthetic build near 0.4 ms; constant-pressure case has no build delay.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Dynamic pressure history shifts the time at which potential-core length and Mach-disk dimensions turn toward steady behavior.

## 12. Quantitative Results

Stable averages: potential-core length 19.5 mm, Mach-disk width 0.67 mm, distance 4.21 mm. Centerline peak Mach about 4.6.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Author interpretation: internal injector filling controls outlet pressure history, which drives the moving shock topology and entrainment evolution.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Tank supply → internal p_in(t) build → time-varying NPR → dynamic shock cells/Mach disk → potential-core and entrainment response.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

NA — nonreacting H2.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Pressure definitions and injector schematic p.3; measured build p.5; three LES histories p.7; shock evolution pp.8–10; conclusions p.11.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

## 18. Limitations
