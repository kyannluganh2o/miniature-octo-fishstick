## 2. Tier 1 Role

Modern mixed-method bridge from near-field compressible shocks to far-field hydrogen-jet evolution.

## 3. Research Question

How do NPR, nozzle-hole diameter, and ambient temperature jointly control near-field shock/vortex structures and far-field hydrogen-jet dispersion?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

CVCC schlieren experiments plus high-resolution near-field LES and far-field RANS in CONVERGE.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

NPR sweep at D=1 mm, 300 K; diameter sweep 0.75-2 mm at 50/5 bar; temperature sweep 300-800 K at 50/5 bar and D=1 mm.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

Six explicit injection/ambient pressure pairs yield NPR 5,10,15,20,40,100. Pressure reference type is not independently stated and remains reported-unspecified.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Low NPR evolves from diamond pattern to weak disk; high NPR creates a full barrel-shock/Mach-disk system, bow shock, strong shear shocks, and secondary vortices.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

NA: gaseous hydrogen jet; no liquid spray droplets despite use of spray terminology for macroscopic jet contours.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

NPR is stored as a contextual ratio. High-NPR near-field regions reach reported Ma>2.5, but no scalar Mach record is created from figure-only fields.

## 10. Time Scales

Near-field evolution is assessed at 8-20 us; far-field histories extend to 1 ms and remain figure-only except explicit text values.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

NPR dominates momentum/shock structure; diameter controls shear-layer scale; temperature is secondary and mainly changes boundary instability/lateral expansion.

## 12. Quantitative Results

At NPR100 radial penetration exceeds 40 mm at 1 ms. Paper-specific 20 us fits are Lm/D=0.6361 NPR^0.5076 and Dm/D=0.2631 NPR^0.6126.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Authors link intensified shock/vortex interactions to entrainment and far-field spreading, with distinct roles for NPR, diameter, and ambient temperature.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

NPR/nozzle/temperature -> near-field shock and shear organization -> secondary vortices/entrainment -> penetration and dispersion.
