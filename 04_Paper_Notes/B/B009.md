# B009

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

- Title: Large eddy simulation of highly turbulent under-expanded hydrogen and methane jets for gaseous-fuelled internal combustion engines
- Paper ID / source record: B009 / B-H2-04
- Original PDF: `02_PDF_Raw/B/B-H2-04_2014_Hamzehloo_LES_Underexpanded_Hydrogen_MethaneJets.pdf`

## 2. Tier 1 Role

Primary case-specific LES test of NPR, gas species, Mach-disk transience, mixing, and penetration.

## 3. Research Question

How do NPR and gas species change the shock structure and mixing of small-nozzle underexpanded gaseous jets?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Compressible LES of five gas/NPR cases in STAR-CCM+ with validation against literature measurements and simulations.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

Converging nozzle D=1.5 mm; refined near field to about 6.7D and computational domain covering 20D downstream.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

Hydrogen NPR=8.5,10,30,70 and methane NPR=8.5; ambient 98.37 kPa and 296 K; storage gas 295.4 K.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Prandtl-Meyer fans, barrel shock, Mach disk, reflected shock, triple points, slip lines, and bow shock are resolved. Mach-disk dimensions are tabulated by case.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

NA: gaseous jets only; no liquid droplets.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

NPR is explicitly P0/P_infinity. Jet-exit and local in-nozzle Mach numbers are stored separately with injector-fixed context.

## 10. Time Scales

For H2 NPR=10, first Mach-disk appearance occurs about 13 us after start and semi-steady structure near 49.6 us; figure-only histories are registered without digitization.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Mach-disk size grows with NPR and overshoots during early transience. Hydrogen mixes before the disk whereas methane does not at matched NPR; main mixing is downstream near jet boundaries.

## 12. Quantitative Results

Table 3: H2 Mach-disk height/width = 2.78/1.091, 3.09/1.34, 5.65/3.37 mm for NPR 8.5/10/30; H2 NPR70 height 8.72 mm and no downstream cell width; CH4 NPR8.5 = 2.85/1.218 mm.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Authors link gas-specific-heat ratio to shock geometry and attribute H2 pre-disk mixing to stronger turbulence/Gortler vortices.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

NPR/gas properties -> transient shock geometry -> shear/turbulence distribution -> mixing location -> penetration and mixture richness.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

Nonreacting study; engine relevance is mixture preparation. Higher NPR enriches local mixture but does not guarantee greater penetration.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

## 18. Limitations
