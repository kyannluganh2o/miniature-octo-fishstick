# C025

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

C025 / C-ST-05. Identity verified on canonical PDF.

## 2. Tier 1 Role

Primary 3-D numerical breakup-timescale and statistics evidence.

## 3. Research Question

How do shock Mach number and instability morphology affect atomization timing and secondary-droplet statistics?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Adaptive three-dimensional VOF simulations for Ms=2 and 3.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

100 um water droplet; ambient air 1 atm and 300 K; finest cell 0.781 um.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

Post-shock inflow supplied analytically for each Ms; droplet initialized at 300 K plus Laplace pressure.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Uniform planar shock followed by analytic post-shock inflow.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

Single 100 um water droplet.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

Reported We=822 and 3760; tau uses post-shock Ur and rho_g.

## 10. Time Scales

Shock transit tau~0.01; instabilities ~0.275; ligament shedding ~0.38; piercing ~1; primary largely atomized ~2.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Both cases deform similarly in tau but piercing modes differ and affect later atomization.

## 12. Quantitative Results

At tau=2, atomized mass below 5 um is 61% for Ms2 and 71% for Ms3; bimodal peaks near 0.004 and 0.01 d0.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Authors connect windward piercing/ligament fracture to the end of the induction interval and rapid mass transfer.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Shock -> flattening/instabilities -> ligaments -> piercing -> secondary breakup cascade.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

No phase change; relevance is availability of small droplets for detonation systems.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Eq.13; Figs.2-11; Conclusions.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

Reported values only; no figure digitization or generated post-shock values.

## 18. Limitations

Two cases, one diameter; smallest sizes grid-limited; run ends before long-time convergence; equilibrium model may accelerate breakup.

## 19. Evidence Candidates

T1-C025-C01 and T1-C025-C02.

## 20. Contradictions / Comparison Hooks
