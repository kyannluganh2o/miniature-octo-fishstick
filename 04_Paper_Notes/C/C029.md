# C029

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

- Title: Numerical study of the transcritical shock-droplet interaction
- Paper ID / source record: C029 / C-PC-03
- Original PDF: `02_PDF_Raw/C/C-PC-03_2021_Boyd_Transcritical_ShockDroplet_Interaction.pdf`

## 2. Tier 1 Role

Tier 1 transcritical shock-droplet pillar bridging classical liquid SDI and gas-bubble SBI behavior.

## 3. Research Question

How do thermodynamic state, pressure and incident Mach control shock refraction, vorticity, deformation and breakup near the Widom line?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

2D axisymmetric inviscid diffuse-interface simulation with a modified Peng-Robinson EoS and hybrid conservative/double-flux solver.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

5 cm n-dodecane droplet in nitrogen; domain and grid are reported; temperature, pressure and Mach are varied independently.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

Preshock temperature 500-800 K, pressure 2-10 MPa, incident shock Mach 1.2-2.0. Pressure basis is thermodynamic but not relabeled absolute/gauge.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

SoS ratio n=cS/cD governs diverging/converging refraction; positive impedance mismatch persists, distinguishing TSDI from classic SBI.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

n-dodecane initially spans liquidlike to gaslike supercritical states; surface tension is neglected in the solver and approximated only for a Weber estimate.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

Incident Mach is laboratory-frame shock Mach. We uses postshock density/velocity, D0 and approximate sigma. Named SoS ratios use explicit operands.

## 10. Time Scales

Shock impact starts t=0; breakup time t* is normalized by postshock velocity, D0 and density ratio. Circulation histories are figure-only.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

n=1 separates diverging and converging behavior; strong convergence creates focal pressure/axial jetting; Mach changes circulation and morphology.

## 12. Quantitative Results

Tables I-III report temperature, pressure and Mach case states. Selected components and thresholds are atomized with exact source locators.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Widom-line state change modifies SoS ratio; shock convergence establishes focal pressure; baroclinic deposition seeds vorticity and interfacial instability.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Thermodynamic state -> refraction/convergence -> pressure/vorticity field -> interface instability and droplet deformation.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

The paper links early transcritical breakup/mixing physics to high-pressure propulsion, but does not model ignition or combustion.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Transcritical definition pp.2-3; method pp.3-5; critical ratios pp.5-7; setup pp.7-10; temperature pp.11-20; pressure pp.21-25; Mach pp.25-30; Tables I-III pp.31-32.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

All retained values are source-reported. The approximate Weber number is labeled as author-reported approximation; no external fluid properties were added.

## 18. Limitations

Inviscid 2D axisymmetric early-time model; no surface tension/diffusion/thermal conduction/chemistry; late 3D breakup not resolved.

## 19. Evidence Candidates

## 20. Contradictions / Comparison Hooks
