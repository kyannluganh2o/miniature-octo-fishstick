# C035

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

C035 / C-MD-03. Identity verified from canonical PDF.

## 2. Tier 1 Role

Primary tandem-droplet interaction experiment.

## 3. Research Question

How do Weber number and tandem spacing alter lead/trailing deformation and breakup?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Planar shock-tube shadowgraphy of two water droplets.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

Approximately 2 mm droplets; seven S/d0 values 1.2-10.5; We 13, 24, 70, 180.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

Post-shock velocity/density estimated from measured shock speed and initial atmosphere; no pressure-ratio record applies.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Planar incident shock; approximately 4.5 ms steady-flow window before expansion waves.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

Two equal droplets aligned with flow; on-center spacing explicitly defined.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

We, Oh and Re are case-specific; definitions preserved.

## 10. Time Scales

T=t ug/(d0 sqrt(rho_d/rho_g)); each droplet zeroed at its own impact, offset below 0.06 ms.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Lead response is nearly isolated-like; trailing deformation and breakup weaken with shielding and can change to puncture/coalescence at close spacing.

## 12. Quantitative Results

Table II retains Tini values; critical S falls from 10.8 at We13 to 5.4 at We180.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Authors attribute attenuation to the lower windward pressure in the lead-droplet wake.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Shock -> lead deformation/wake -> trailing shielding -> delayed/weaker deformation and altered breakup.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

NA: nonreacting water droplets.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Eqs.1-6; Tables I-II; Figs.3-4 and 18-20; Conclusion.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

Reported values only; no map or curve digitization.

## 18. Limitations

Low-We rupture extends beyond steady window; shadowgraph projection; spacing matrix partly figure-only.

## 20. Contradictions / Comparison Hooks
