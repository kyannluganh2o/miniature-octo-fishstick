# C024

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

C024 / C-ST-04. Identity verified from canonical PDF.

## 2. Tier 1 Role

Primary small-droplet breakup/evaporation experiment with model testing.

## 3. Research Question

How do micrometre droplets break, disperse and evaporate after a strong shock, and which simplified breakup model reproduces the cloud?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Shock-tube acetone droplet curtain; PDPA, shadowgraphy, Mie/PLIF; CDC model.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

Incident M=2.09+/-0.01; post-shock velocity 461.3+/-4.6 m/s.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

Initial dry air about 1 atm/293 K; acetone droplets in nitrogen/acetone vapor at 1 atm/293 K.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Planar incident shock crosses a dilute droplet curtain.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

D10=14.16 um and D32=37.3 um; initial droplet interactions negligible at less than 0.01% liquid volume.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

We=662+/-19 and 1743+/-49; Re=1647+/-21 and 4338+/-56; Oh=0.02 and 0.01.

## 10. Time Scales

Breakup 0.4-8.4 us, cloud growth 8.4-98 us, evaporation-dominated stage 98-253 us; last clouds 250-300 us.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Small droplets equilibrate quickly; size-dependent lag stretches the cloud and evaporation removes the dispersed children.

## 12. Quantitative Results

Explicit conditions and times are stored; curves remain figure-only.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Authors connect cloud length to breakup duration, parent drag and child-size distribution; KHRT points to strong RT growth.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Shock -> deformation/breakup -> size-dependent child lag/cloud growth -> evaporation.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

Nonreacting acetone; vapor saturation is included in the simplified evaporation model.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

Reported records only; no curve digitization and no derived values.

## 18. Limitations

Individual deformation unresolved; simplified 1-D gas and cloud modeling; polydisperse curtain.
