# C019

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

C019 / C-SD-12. Title and authors verified on PDF p.1; canonical raw PDF unchanged.

## 2. Tier 1 Role

Primary numerical curvature-response evidence.

## 3. Research Question

How does divergent-shock curvature alter droplet deformation, K-H inception and global dynamics?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

Two-dimensional compressible two-phase simulations of a cylindrical water droplet; divergent and planar shocks compared.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

D0=4.8 mm; Ma0=2.4 and 3.2; omega=R0/RD=4, 6, 8 and planar limit.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

Droplet and unshocked gas initially 300 K and 101325 Pa.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Incident/reflected/transmitted waves, Mach reflection, internal rarefaction/compression reflections, and diffraction are resolved.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

Single cylindrical water droplet; capillary and viscous forces omitted.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

Ma0 and omega are explicitly contextualized; validation discussion gives We and Re only for a separate check, not merged into main cases.

## 10. Time Scales

t*=t ug/(RD sqrt(rho_l/rho_g)); t0 is first windward contact; wave establishment is approximately t*<=0.10.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

Divergent curvature moves ligaments toward the front, strengthens spanwise extension, slows streamwise response and weakens fragmentation relative to planar loading.

## 12. Quantitative Results

Table 1 preserves theta_f for eight Ma0/curvature cases; the main case rests near theta_f=48.46 degrees at t* about 0.15.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Authors attribute K-H inception to the fixed point formed by combined external and internal impingement and later exposed mainly to shear.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Divergent shock -> altered tangential/normal loading -> fixed-point migration/K-H inception -> ligament deformation and fragmentation.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

NA: nonreacting calculation without mixing, ignition or combustion.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Key sources: Fig.1; Eq.2.5; Figs.5, 9, 11; Eqs.4.4-4.8; Table 1; Conclusion.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

All numeric records are reported. No derived or inferred numeric records and no figure digitization.

## 18. Limitations

Two-dimensional cylindrical geometry; no viscosity, surface tension, phase change, or three-dimensional curvature tensor effects.
