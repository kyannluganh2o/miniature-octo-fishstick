# C018

## 1. Bibliographic Identity

- Title: Exploration of shock-droplet interaction based on high-fidelity simulation and improved theoretical model
- Paper ID / source record: C018 / C-SD-11
- Original PDF: `02_PDF_Raw/C/C-SD-11_2024_Xiong_ShockDroplet_HighFidelitySimulation_TheoreticalModel.pdf`

## 2. Tier 1 Role

High-fidelity primary test of early internal-wave and pressure mechanisms across low/high gas-liquid wave-speed ratios.

## 3. Research Question

Does reflected-expansion-wave focusing identify the NPP/cavitation site across liquid properties and shock strengths, and how must the theory change at high wave-speed ratio?

## 4. Study Type and Configuration

2D high-order compressible multiphase simulation with MFC plus improved ray-tracing model; cylindrical water/n-hexane droplets.

## 5. Geometry and Operating Conditions

Planar incident shock, half-domain 14D0 x 6D0, 800 cells/D0, reflective symmetry boundary.

## 6. Injection / Ambient Conditions

Pre-shock air and droplet start at atmospheric pressure. Ms spans 1.75-8.4; Table 1 preserves ppost/ppre, rhopost/rhopre, and n for both liquids.

## 7. Wave / Jet Structure

Incident, transmitted, reflected, expansion waves and Mach stem form an internal wave envelope whose topology changes with n.

## 8. Droplet / Spray Initial Conditions

Water and n-hexane droplets; densities 1000 and 657 kg/m3 at NTP. No phase-transition model.

## 9. Dimensionless Parameters

Ms is incident shock Mach in laboratory frame. We=rho_post u_post^2 D0/sigma and Re=rho_post u_post D0/mu; ranges and source-limited components are linked.

## 10. Time Scales

NPP history is figure-only; characteristic nondimensional occurrence is near t*=1.25, slightly earlier at higher Ms.

## 11. Main Physical Observations

At low n the NPP and focus positions align; at high n the NPP tends toward a constant while theoretical focus shifts. Mach stem must be included.

## 12. Quantitative Results

Table 1 contains 12 Ms cases, pressure/density jump ratios, and liquid-specific n values; all are retained case-by-case.

## 13. Mechanism Proposed by Authors

Internal pressure evolution contains shock, relaxation, fluctuation, and expansion-wave contributions. Expansion focusing alone does not determine total NPP.

## 14. Wave → Droplet/Spray Response Chain

Incident/Mach-stem forcing -> transmitted/reflected wave envelope -> expansion contribution plus relaxation/fluctuation -> total NPP -> potential cavitation site.

## 15. Mixing / Ignition / Combustion Consequences

No ignition/combustion; relevance is early pressure mechanism and possible cavitation/breakup consequences.

## 16. Key Figures, Tables, and Equations

Configuration p.4; Table 1 and We/Re p.5; Table 2 p.6; NPP history p.9; NPP/focus trend p.13; improved model pp.20-22; factors pp.23-28; conclusion pp.28-29.

## 17. Reported vs Derived vs Inferred

All case values are reported. No external property completion, project derivation, or figure digitization.

## 18. Limitations

2D cylindrical geometry; no phase transition; cavitation location is inferred from NPP rather than directly resolved; model uses fitted Mach-stem trajectory.

## 19. Evidence Candidates

T1-C018-C01 to C03 separate correlation, model correction, and multi-factor pressure mechanism.

## 20. Contradictions / Comparison Hooks

Provides rigorous early-stage mechanism constraints for shock-droplet pressure histories.
