# D001 Tier3 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T3-D001-C01

Candidate claim: High ambient temperature and density suppressed diesel-spray shock generation by raising sound speed and decelerating the spray during injection-rate ramp-up; typical near-TDC conditions were therefore unlikely to produce spray shocks.

Evidence type: reported result

Evidence summary: Schlieren distinguished attached/detached shocks from weak acoustic waves across the ambient matrix.

Source locator: LOC-D001-0002

Support type: direct_observation

Limitations: 150 MPa single-hole injector and vessel conditions; conventional multi-hole injectors have slower ramp-up.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-D001-0001; PAR-D001-0002

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-D001-001

Potential chapter role: Boundary conditions for injector-generated shocks in engines.
