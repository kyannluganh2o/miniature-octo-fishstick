# A001 Tier3 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T3-A001-C01

Candidate claim: In this HPDI engine, raising gas injection pressure reduced PM mainly at high load, while low-load emissions and fuel consumption were comparatively insensitive.

Evidence type: reported result

Evidence summary: Across the tested map, high-load PM mass/number fell and combustion duration shortened; effects diminished with engine speed.

Source locator: LOC-A001-0002

Support type: direct_observation

Limitations: Engine- and operating-map-specific; multiple higher-order interactions were significant.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-A001-0001; PAR-A001-0002

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-A001-001

Potential chapter role: Historical context for load-dependent pressure benefits.
