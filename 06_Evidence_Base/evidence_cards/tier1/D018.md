# D018 Tier 1 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T1-D018-C01

Candidate claim: Detonation-driven RP-3 and water breakup proceeds from a KHI-dominated stripping stage to coupled KHI-RTI piercing and catastrophic breakup.

Evidence type: primary_experimental

Evidence summary: High-speed sequences resolve early shear stripping and later RT piercing for both liquids.

Source locator: LOC-D018-0005

Support type: direct_observation

Limitations: Shadowgraph is a two-dimensional projection and the smallest early structures are below resolution.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-D018-0048; PAR-D018-0057

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-D018-001

Potential chapter role: Detonation-induced breakup mechanism.

## Candidate Claim 2

Claim ID: T1-D018-C02

Candidate claim: Shock-derived deformation and drag correlations are not directly transferable to detonation loading because the post-detonation adverse pressure gradient reduces convection and aerodynamic forcing.

Evidence type: experiment_plus_model_comparison

Evidence summary: Detonation data collapse to dc/d0=1+0.72821t* and Cd=0.23, far below the cited shock-induced slopes/drag.

Source locator: LOC-D018-0006

Support type: author_interpretation

Limitations: Cross-study shock comparison is secondary; primary detonation correlations are measured in this facility.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-D018-0073; PAR-D018-0074

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-D018-002

Potential chapter role: Loading-history dependence and model transferability.

## Candidate Claim 3

Claim ID: T1-D018-C03

Candidate claim: Collapsed detonation breakup time differs by liquid: t*b=10.06 for water and 7.90 for RP-3, with surface tension implicated in the difference.

Evidence type: primary_experimental_correlation

Evidence summary: Both case families fall within the fitted prediction bands and adapted mass-stripping equations use the two constants.

Source locator: LOC-D018-0008

Support type: experimental_correlation

Limitations: The time normalization itself excludes surface tension; the mechanism is an author interpretation supported by the liquid comparison.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-D018-0075; PAR-D018-0076; PAR-D018-0002; PAR-D018-0003

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-D018-003

Potential chapter role: Detonation breakup-time scaling.
