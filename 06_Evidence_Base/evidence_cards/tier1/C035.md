# C035 Tier 1 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T1-C035-C01

Candidate claim: Lead-droplet wake shielding delays and weakens the trailing droplet response, especially at smaller spacing and lower Weber number.

Evidence type: primary_experimental

Evidence summary: The trailing droplet has later initial deformation, smaller cross-stream maximum and lower mean drag while the lead droplet remains nearly isolated-like.

Source locator: LOC-C035-0006

Support type: direct_observation

Limitations: Low-We rupture extends beyond the steady post-shock window; exact seven-spacing matrix is figure based.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-C035-0003; PAR-C035-0024; PAR-C035-0004; PAR-C035-0007

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-C035-001

Potential chapter role: Multi-droplet shielding and dense-spray breakup.
