# A007 Tier 1 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T1-A007-C01

Candidate claim: Stronger temporal and spatial overlap can delay or suppress diesel-pilot ignition while promoting faster natural-gas ignition, producing an interaction-dependent trade-off.

Evidence type: primary_experimental

Evidence summary: Shadowgraph operability and ignition-delay maps show opposing pilot and gas responses across timing and angle.

Source locator: LOC-A007-0005

Support type: experimental_correlation

Limitations: Single RCEM operating point, two separate single-hole injectors, no swirl or neighboring jets.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-A007-0009; PAR-A007-0006; PAR-A007-0005

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-A007-001; PROC-A007-002; INT-A007-001

Potential chapter role: Pilot-main-gas chronology and interaction mechanism.

## Candidate Claim 2

Claim ID: T1-A007-C02

Candidate claim: The time of natural-gas ignition relative to gas SOI organizes heat-release shape because it represents the achieved level of premixing.

Evidence type: primary_experimental

Evidence summary: Peak and mean HRR plus combustion-duration trends collapse by ignition time relative to gas SOI.

Source locator: LOC-A007-0006

Support type: experimental_correlation

Limitations: HRR is model-derived and hot-product heat losses are neglected; curves were not digitized.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-A007-0008; PAR-A007-0010

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-A007-003; HIST-A007-001

Potential chapter role: Temporal control of premixing and combustion phasing.
