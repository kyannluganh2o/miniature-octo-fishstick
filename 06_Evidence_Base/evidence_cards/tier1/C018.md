# C018 Tier 1 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T1-C018-C01

Candidate claim: Focus-point and negative-peak-pressure locations align at low gas-liquid wave-speed ratio but diverge at high ratio.

Evidence type: simulation_resolved

Evidence summary: Complete Ms/liquid case matrix shows NPP location becoming approximately constant at high n while the theoretical focus continues its decreasing trend.

Source locator: LOC-C018-0009

Support type: simulation_resolved

Limitations: Five-equation model has no phase transition, so cavitation itself is not resolved.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-C018-0015; PAR-C018-0063; PAR-C018-0019; PAR-C018-0055

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-C018-001; HIST-C018-002

Potential chapter role: Internal-wave mechanism and cavitation-location caution.

## Candidate Claim 2

Claim ID: T1-C018-C02

Candidate claim: Including Mach-stem motion improves the theoretical internal-wave envelope for high wave-speed-ratio cases.

Evidence type: model_based

Evidence summary: Ray-tracing model switches the disturbance initiator from incident shock to Mach stem where appropriate.

Source locator: LOC-C018-0007

Support type: model_based

Limitations: Two-dimensional cylindrical geometry and fitted Mach-stem trajectory.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-C018-0019; PAR-C018-0055

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-C018-002

Potential chapter role: High-ratio correction to internal-wave models.

## Candidate Claim 3

Claim ID: T1-C018-C03

Candidate claim: The negative peak pressure results from the combined shock, relaxation, fluctuation, and expansion-wave effects rather than expansion-wave focusing alone.

Evidence type: simulation_resolved

Evidence summary: Pressure histories and wave-count comparison show the focus maximizes the expansion-wave contribution, while NPP has the largest total pressure drop.

Source locator: LOC-C018-0009

Support type: simulation_resolved

Limitations: No cavitation phase transition; pressure-factor decomposition requires further validation.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-C018-0007; PAR-C018-0008

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-C018-003; EVT-C018-002; HIST-C018-001

Potential chapter role: Multi-contribution pressure mechanism in early shock-droplet interaction.
