## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T1-C025-C01

Candidate claim: Mach 2 and 3 cases show similar nondimensional deformation timing but different piercing morphology and subsequent atomized-mass transfer.

Source locator: LOC-C025-0006

Support type: simulation_resolved

Limitations: Only two shock strengths and one initial diameter; thermomechanical equilibration is quasi-instantaneous.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-C025-0006; PAR-C025-0007; PAR-C025-0008; PAR-C025-0009; PAR-C025-0011; PAR-C025-0012

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-C025-001
