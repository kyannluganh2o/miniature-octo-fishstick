# B021 Tier 1 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T1-B021-C01

Candidate claim: At fixed nozzle geometry, increasing contextual NPR shifts the Mach disk downstream and increases its diameter; NPR-dependent correlations reduce error relative to constant-coefficient forms.

Evidence type: primary_numerical

Evidence summary: NPR 2.5-60 sweep and Tables 2-3 quantify Mach-disk dimensions and correlation error.

Source locator: LOC-B021-0004

Support type: simulation_resolved

Limitations: RANS, convergent nozzle, selected gas/EoS and steady axisymmetric configuration.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-B021-0004; PAR-B021-0006; PAR-B021-0007; PAR-B021-0008; PAR-B021-0009

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-B021-001

Potential chapter role: Contextual NPR to near-field shock scaling.

## Candidate Claim 2

Claim ID: T1-B021-C02

Candidate claim: For the modeled ICE pressure history, the slow NPR variation produces no practically significant difference between steady and unsteady Mach-disk position or hydrogen distribution.

Evidence type: primary_numerical

Evidence summary: URANS versus steady comparisons show at most about 2% Mach-disk-position difference for the stated variation rate.

Source locator: LOC-B021-0006

Support type: simulation_resolved

Limitations: Result depends on the modeled NPR variation rate and should not be generalized to faster transients.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-B021-0010; PAR-B021-0011; PAR-B021-0012

Relevant process/event IDs: HIST-B021-001; HIST-B021-002; INT-B021-001

Potential chapter role: Transient versus quasi-steady ICE applicability boundary.

## Candidate Claim 3

Claim ID: T1-B021-C03

Candidate claim: Higher chamber temperature enhances hydrogen diffusion without changing Mach-disk position at fixed NPR, while closely spaced parallel jets develop an expansion-wave-linked low-pressure region that bends jets together.

Evidence type: primary_numerical

Evidence summary: Temperature comparison and 1/2 mm inter-axis simulations separate gas-dynamic structure from diffusion and multi-jet interaction.

Source locator: LOC-B021-0008

Support type: simulation_resolved

Limitations: Multi-jet analysis is preliminary; parallel geometry and NPR 10 only.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-B021-0013; PAR-B021-0014; PAR-B021-0015; PAR-B021-0016

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-B021-002; PROC-B021-003

Potential chapter role: ICE temperature and multi-hole interaction effects.
