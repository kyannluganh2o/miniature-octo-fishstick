# C029 Tier 1 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T1-C029-C01

Candidate claim: At supercritical pressure, crossing from liquidlike to gaslike n-dodecane changes the speed-of-sound ratio and drives a transition from diverging to converging refracted-shock behavior at n=1.

Evidence type: primary_numerical

Evidence summary: Temperature sweep at 6 MPa links thermodynamic state, SoS ratio and observed shock topology.

Source locator: LOC-C029-0006

Support type: simulation_resolved

Limitations: Idealized inviscid 2D axisymmetric early-time model; no surface tension, diffusion, thermal conduction, or chemistry.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-C029-0005; PAR-C029-0006; PAR-C029-0007; PAR-C029-0010

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-C029-001; HIST-C029-001

Potential chapter role: Transcritical state-to-wave-structure mechanism.

## Candidate Claim 2

Claim ID: T1-C029-C02

Candidate claim: Strongly convergent TSDI produces an internal focal pressure peak and outward axial jet, while the weak/strong convergence transition occurs near n=1.48 in the simulated system.

Evidence type: primary_numerical

Evidence summary: Pressure and shock images distinguish inward/weak and outward/strong axial-jet cases.

Source locator: LOC-C029-0008

Support type: simulation_resolved

Limitations: Threshold estimate uses simplified path/curvature assumptions and is not universal without validation.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-C029-0010; PAR-C029-0015; PAR-C029-0008

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-C029-002

Potential chapter role: Shock focusing and internal-pressure mechanism.

## Candidate Claim 3

Claim ID: T1-C029-C03

Candidate claim: Increasing shock strength raises baroclinic circulation and downstream pressure-gradient-driven inward deformation while reducing overall radial flattening in the tested TSDI cases.

Evidence type: primary_numerical

Evidence summary: Mach 1.2-2.0 sweep at fixed thermodynamic state separates shock-strength effects from SoS-ratio effects.

Source locator: LOC-C029-0009

Support type: simulation_resolved

Limitations: Later three-dimensional breakup is outside the modeled scope.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-C029-0009; PAR-C029-0016; PAR-C029-0014

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-C029-003; HIST-C029-002

Potential chapter role: Shock-strength control of morphology and circulation.
