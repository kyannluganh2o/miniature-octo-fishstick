# A026 Tier 1 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T1-A026-C01

Candidate claim: Increasing diesel energy share strengthens and spreads the pilot flame, accelerating the initial H2 reaction, while the late mixing-controlled phase remains similar across energy shares.

Evidence type: primary_experimental

Evidence summary: Pressure/aHRR and flame images show a larger pilot and faster early H2 flame development as H2 share decreases from 95% to 70%.

Source locator: LOC-A026-0006

Support type: direct_observation

Limitations: Natural-luminosity thresholds and two imaging arrangements limit quantitative cross-sweep intensity comparison.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-A026-0006; PAR-A026-0009; PAR-A026-0022

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-A026-001

Potential chapter role: Pilot-energy control of H2 ignition.

## Candidate Claim 2

Claim ID: T1-A026-C02

Candidate claim: Advancing H2 injection changes the premixed/mixing-controlled balance and the 30 CA bTDC case significantly delays diesel pilot ignition, attributed to preignition dilution.

Evidence type: primary_experimental

Evidence summary: aHRR and flame images separate timing-dependent interaction, premixing and delayed pilot ignition.

Source locator: LOC-A026-0005

Support type: experimental_correlation

Limitations: Fuel distributions before ignition were not directly visualized; dilution is author interpretation.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-A026-0018; PAR-A026-0021; PAR-A026-0023

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-A026-002; PROC-A026-003

Potential chapter role: Injection-event chain and pilot/H2 coupling.
