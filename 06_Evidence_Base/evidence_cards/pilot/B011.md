# B011 Pilot Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: PILOT-B011-C01

Claim: At fixed NPR, changing ambient pressure primarily alters starting-transient timing, not the settled near-nozzle shock geometry.

Evidence type: simulation_resolved

Evidence summary: Mach-disk dimensions, reflected-shock angle, and shock-cell spacing are reported as essentially unchanged across ambient pressures.

Source location: PDF p.21, Conclusions

Support type: direct

Value status: reported

Limitations: Applies to the simulated H2 NPR-10 cases.

Potential review use: Defines the reference pressure roles needed before comparing underexpanded H2-jet papers.
