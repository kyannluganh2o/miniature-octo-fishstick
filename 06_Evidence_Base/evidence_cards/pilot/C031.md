# C031 Pilot Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: PILOT-C031-C01

Claim: Evaporation can suppress KH-wave growth through a low-temperature vapor layer that reduces interfacial shear.

Evidence type: simulation_resolved

Evidence summary: Temperature, vorticity and interface morphology are mutually consistent across no-phase-change and evaporation cases.

Source location: PDF p.20, Conclusions

Support type: author_interpretation

Value status: reported

Limitations: Depends on manually selected phase-change coefficients.

Potential review use: Shows that breakup and phase change require coupled state and support-type fields, while preserving model inputs separately.
