# D003 Pilot Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: PILOT-D003-C01

Claim: A jet-induced shock can create a push-away impulse, but the effective shocked-state duration is shortened by a following expansion wave.

Evidence type: model_based

Evidence summary: Measured waves and the 1D model show a thin shocked region and finite momentum-transfer interval.

Source location: PDF p.10, Conclusions

Support type: indirect

Value status: reported

Limitations: Breakup and evaporation were not modeled.

Potential review use: Directly determines the loading-duration taxonomy required before bulk extraction.

## Candidate Claim 2

Claim ID: PILOT-D003-C02

Claim: Droplet response depends strongly on particle size relative to the short forcing interval.

Evidence type: simulation_resolved

Evidence summary: Small droplets approach the gas response; 50 μm droplets have a response time much longer than the shocked-state exposure.

Source location: PDF p.9, Figs. 12–13 and discussion

Support type: direct

Value status: reported

Limitations: Stokes comparison is model-context specific.

Potential review use: Directly determines the loading-duration taxonomy required before bulk extraction.
