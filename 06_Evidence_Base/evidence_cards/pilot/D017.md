# D017 Pilot Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: PILOT-D017-C01

Claim: Evaporation alone cannot explain the observed ~10.9 mm droplet-survival region; concurrent breakup is required in the model interpretation.

Evidence type: model_based

Evidence summary: No-breakup calculations exceed observations by orders of magnitude, whereas breakup models shorten survival into the observed range.

Source location: PDF p.9, Table 4

Support type: indirect

Value status: reported

Limitations: Reduced models assume constant post-shock gas conditions.

Potential review use: Tests joint storage of breakup, evaporation, variable acceleration, cloud morphology and model validity.

## Candidate Claim 2

Claim ID: PILOT-D017-C02

Claim: Existing shock-driven breakup models predict faster extinction than some observed droplets under the detonation's variable acceleration.

Evidence type: author_interpretation

Evidence summary: Clouds persist beyond 15 mm while the largest WERT49 modeled case is ~11 mm.

Source location: PDF p.10, Conclusions

Support type: author_interpretation

Value status: reported

Limitations: Mie scattering has a detection threshold and snapshots are ensemble-based.

Potential review use: Tests joint storage of breakup, evaporation, variable acceleration, cloud morphology and model validity.
