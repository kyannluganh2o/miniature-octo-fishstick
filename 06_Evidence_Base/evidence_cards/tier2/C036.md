# C036 Tier2 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T2-C036-C01

Candidate claim: At low Weber number, decreasing parallel-droplet spacing shifted breakup from bag toward trailing and shuttlecock modes through a spacing-dependent reversal of post-flattening bending.

Evidence type: reported result

Evidence summary: High-speed images and potential-flow pressure distributions support the mode boundary near L/D of order 2 for the tested geometry.

Source locator: LOC-C036-0003

Support type: experimental_correlation

Limitations: The theoretical analogue is two-dimensional cylinders; spherical-droplet boundary may shift.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-C036-0004; PAR-C036-0005; PAR-C036-0006

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-C036-001; PROC-C036-002

Potential chapter role: Parallel-droplet spacing effect at low Weber number.

## Candidate Claim 2

Claim ID: T2-C036-C02

Candidate claim: At higher Weber number, sufficiently small spacing promoted rapid equatorial filament formation and closure of the channel between adjacent droplets, producing a closed breakup mode.

Evidence type: reported result

Evidence summary: High-speed imaging distinguishes open and closed modes; smaller gaps close earlier and can reopen repeatedly.

Source locator: LOC-C036-0004

Support type: direct_observation

Limitations: Finite experimental parameter space and generated-droplet alignment variability.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-C036-0003; PAR-C036-0004; PAR-C036-0005; PAR-C036-0006

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-C036-002

Potential chapter role: Collective breakup topology under shock-driven flow.
