# A012 Tier2 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T2-A012-C01

Candidate claim: At the simulated medium-load HPDI point, the natural-gas injection did not ignite without the diesel pilot, demonstrating a required pilot trigger for that condition.

Evidence type: reported result

Evidence summary: Paired CFD cases differed only by pilot injection; the NG-only case released no heat.

Source locator: LOC-A012-0003

Support type: simulation_resolved

Limitations: Single operating point and model-dependent chemistry/turbulence representation.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-A012-0002; PAR-A012-0003

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-A012-001

Potential chapter role: Numerical isolation of the pilot-trigger function.

## Candidate Claim 2

Claim ID: T2-A012-C02

Candidate claim: In the homogeneous-reactor analysis, temperature dominated methane ignition above about 1100 K, while realistic H/OH radicals shortened ignition delay mainly in the 950–1100 K interval.

Evidence type: reported result

Evidence summary: HRM sensitivity maps varied initial temperature and radical concentration using states sampled from engine CFD.

Source locator: LOC-A012-0003

Support type: model_based

Limitations: Zero-dimensional chemistry isolates kinetics and omits turbulent transport.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-A012-0004

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-A012-002

Potential chapter role: Thermal-versus-radical pathways in pilot-assisted NG ignition.
