# A002 Tier2 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T2-A002-C01

Candidate claim: Increasing natural-gas rail pressure shortened the mixing-controlled combustion event in the tested HPDI engine, with the strongest benefits at high-load/high-speed conditions.

Evidence type: reported result

Evidence summary: The 300-to-600 bar program and CFD comparison associate higher pressure with increased injection rate and improved late-cycle mixing.

Source locator: LOC-A002-0003

Support type: simulation_resolved

Limitations: Single engine architecture; transient-cycle performance was not evaluated.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-A002-0003; PAR-A002-0004

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-A002-001

Potential chapter role: HPDI injection-pressure control and mixing-rate mechanism.

## Candidate Claim 2

Claim ID: T2-A002-C02

Candidate claim: The efficiency and PM advantages of higher gas pressure came with higher combustion harshness and NOx, and nozzle-flow control was proposed as mitigation.

Evidence type: reported result

Evidence summary: Conclusion reports load-dependent efficiency/PM benefits plus combustion-noise and NOx penalties.

Source locator: LOC-A002-0003

Support type: direct_observation

Limitations: Tradeoffs depend on operating point and nozzle design.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-A002-0003

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-A002-002

Potential chapter role: Engineering tradeoffs for high-pressure HPDI.
