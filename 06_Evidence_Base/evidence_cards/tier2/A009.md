# A009 Tier2 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T2-A009-C01

Candidate claim: At lower chamber temperature and pressure, longer pilot ignition delay made pilot ignition more vulnerable to disruption by the adjacent natural-gas jet.

Evidence type: reported result

Evidence summary: Optical imaging showed delayed/downstream pilot ignition and misfire when overlap reduced the free path below lift-off length.

Source locator: LOC-A009-0002

Support type: direct_observation

Limitations: Single free-jet arrangement without wall, swirl, or neighboring-jet effects.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-A009-0003; PAR-A009-0004

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-A009-001

Potential chapter role: Ambient-state control of pilot robustness.
