# A011 Tier2 Evidence Card

## Candidate Claim 1

Claim ID: T2-A011-C01

Candidate claim: Relative pilot/gas injection timing was the primary control on natural-gas premixing and shifted combustion from non-premixed through distributed partially premixed to stratified premixed flame propagation.

Evidence type: reported result

Evidence summary: OH* reaction-zone analysis and HRR jointly resolved the regime transition.

Source locator: LOC-A011-0002

Support type: direct_observation

Limitations: Optical engine had low compression ratio, cylindrical bowl, and line-of-sight imaging.

Relevant parameter IDs: PAR-A011-0003; PAR-A011-0004

Relevant process/event IDs: PROC-A011-001

Potential chapter role: Conceptual staging of HPDI ignition and combustion.

## Candidate Claim 2

Limitations: Results are bounded by the optical engine configuration.
